## Objective

Design, develop and demonstrate a ruggedized, field-deployable vehicle forensics device for non-destructive, rapid data triage and extraction.

## Description

- **Need:** Marine Corps and joint operators doing tactical site exploitation need field hardware and software to get at vehicle-derived data; the topic calls this a critical intelligence gap.
- **Problem:** existing tools are lab-oriented, and infotainment units and ECUs use proprietary architectures, so field teams may have to disassemble the dashboard.
- **Scope:** a low size-weight-and-power, ruggedized, all-in-one device that connects through OBD-II or USB, profiles the vehicle, runs an automated extraction script, and does so without disassembly while keeping forensic integrity.
- **Data:** telematics (GPS, track logs) and informatics (infotainment, paired Bluetooth devices, Wi-Fi cache, cellular pairings) across makes, model years and international manufacturers.
- **Threshold:** representative vehicle compatibility; high-priority extraction in under 15 minutes with verifiable accuracy; portable ruggedized kit.
- **Objective:** pocket-sized plug-and-play device; modular path to broad compatibility; comprehensive extraction in under 5 minutes.

## Phase I

Show feasibility of a mobile, software-driven vehicle forensics capability that avoids teardown. Deliverables: a feasibility study and initial architecture; projected extraction speeds, metrics and limits; a detailed Phase II plan with milestones and risk mitigation.

## Phase II

A field-ready prototype with broader compatibility and deeper extraction. Deliverables: a functional prototype ready for government evaluation; demonstrations in an operationally relevant environment; full technical documentation; a commercialization and transition plan.

## Phase III and dual use

Integration into Forensics Dominance System - Marine Corps (FDS-MC) tactical site exploitation kits; also USSOCOM exploitation programs and other forensic intelligence elements. Commercial: law enforcement roadside and crime-scene extraction, insurance fraud investigation, crash reconstruction, and fleet telematics review.
